title: Ending someone's access while they save
area: Tickets
link: desk:request-list
---
Deactivating a person, or removing a role, capability, team or portal site, now waits for a change they are saving, and anything they start afterwards is refused. Importing external identifiers no longer fails with an error when someone adds a link at the same moment.
