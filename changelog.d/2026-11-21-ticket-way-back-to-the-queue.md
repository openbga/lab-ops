title: A ticket's way back to the queue keeps your latest list settings
area: Tickets
link: desk:request-list
---
Going back to Tickets from a ticket, after changing several tickets, or by cancelling Save view now brings back only the filters, sort and rows per page you chose on the list, so a sort or page size you changed in another tab is not undone. Previous in list and Next in list now also appear when you open a ticket from the list without choosing a filter or sort, and a view saved from the list keeps the order it showed, even when that is the default order.
